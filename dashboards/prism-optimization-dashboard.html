DFS Heuristic Scoring Function

score = (expected_gain_per_gpu / cost_minutes) × (1 − accuracy_risk) × (1 − crash_risk) × target_gap_multiplier

The orchestrator maintains a priority stack. After each action completes, all remaining candidates are re-scored and the highest-scored is popped next. Exploration is depth-first along the most promising branch, with backtracking when scores shift.

Score update rules: Gain >0% → boost similar actions by 1.5×. Gain ≤0% → reduce by 0.5×. After 2+ backend wins → push combined_test at sum×1.5. After kernel keep → push re-profile. After 2+ discards on vendor kernels → reduce all GEAK scores to ~0. When all scores <1.0 → proceed to sweep → report.
